MoolKhoj Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

MoolKhoj is a browser extension that helps Meesho sellers research products. This policy explains what data it handles. In short: everything stays in your own browser, and nothing is sent to us.

What the extension reads

  • Product information that meesho.com shows on the pages you open: product name, price, rating, number of reviews, listing date, category, seller name and state.
  • The public "More information" section of products on your screen (manufacturer name and address, seller name), read one product at a time from Meesho.
  • Numbers you type yourself, such as buy price, packing cost and profit margin.
  • An ads report file, only if you choose one. It is read inside your browser.

What it never reads

  • Your Meesho account, login, cart, orders, addresses or payment details.
  • Pages on any website other than meesho.com.
  • Personal data of shoppers or reviewers.

Where the data is kept

All data is stored in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer. MoolKhoj has no server. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and it uses no analytics, tracking or advertising code. Files you download (CSV) are saved only where you choose.

Sharing and sale

We do not collect your data, so we do not sell, share or transfer it. It is not used for any purpose other than showing you the research features of the extension, and never for credit or lending decisions.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension from your browser deletes all data it stored. You can also delete all saved products from the extension's Settings tab.

Meesho

MoolKhoj is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meesho. Your use of meesho.com is governed by Meesho's own terms and privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: moolkhoj@gmail.com
